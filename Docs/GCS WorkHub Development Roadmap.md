# GCS WorkHub Development Roadmap
## Phased Development Guide

### Purpose

This document provides a phased roadmap for building the GCS WorkHub platform. The focus is to ensure that each phase delivers usable business value while creating a foundation for future expansion. The objective is to avoid building an overly complex system at the beginning and instead grow the platform in a controlled and sustainable manner.

# Success Criteria

The platform can be considered successful when:

- Every employee understands what they are responsible for.
- Every task has a clear owner and deadline.
- Every department can monitor its workload and performance.
- Management can identify risks before they become problems.
- Work no longer depends on manual follow-up.
- Operational visibility exists across the entire company.
- GCS has a single source of truth for work, projects, performance, and accountability.

---

# Recommended Build Sequence

1. Phase 1 – Foundation & Core Work Management
2. Phase 2 – Projects, Approvals & Workflow Control
3. Phase 3 – Automation & Notifications
4. Phase 4 – Workload & Resource Management
5. Phase 5 – Performance Management & KPIs
6. Phase 6 – Client & Business Operations Management
7. Phase 7 – Strategic Management & Intelligence
8. Phase 8 – AI-Powered WorkHub

This sequence ensures that GCS first establishes operational discipline before introducing automation, analytics, intelligence, and AI.
