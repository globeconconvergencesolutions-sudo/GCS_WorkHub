# GCS WorkHub — Roles, responsibilities, and what you can do

**Audience:** everyone who will use GCS WorkHub.  
**How to use this:** read the overview, then the chapter for your role. Keep the full document — it is useful to understand what the person next to you can see and why a button may be missing on your account.

WorkHub is the shared operational workspace for Globcons Consulting Services. It is not five different apps. It is one workspace with **five desks**. Your desk is set by your **primary role**. You sign in with the email you were given. A starter password is issued when you are invited; change it after first sign-in if your administrator asks you to.

If something you need is missing, it is usually a role or placement issue (wrong department, or the wrong desk). Ask the **workspace administrator**, not a colleague, to correct it.

---

## 1. The five desks at a glance

| Desk | Role in WorkHub | Job at Globecon | Typical landing screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace Administrator | `admin` | Keep the company structure honest: departments, teams, who sits where, who has which desk | **Home** (company cockpit) |
| Managing Director | `managing_director` | Run the company from one place: all work, all people, company reports | **Home** |
| Department Head | `department_head` | Run one function: people and work in that department | **Departments** |
| Manager | `manager` | Assign and move work in that department | **Departments** |
| Employee | `employee` | Do the work assigned to you; ask for more work when you need it | **My tasks** |

**One person, one primary role.** A department head is not also an employee on the same account. The administrator and the managing director are **different people** with **different rights**.

## 2. Screens you will see

Not every role sees every item in the sidebar.

| Screen | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Company cockpit: overdue and stuck work, department scorecard, leadership requests, reminders. Admin and MD only. |
| **Overview** | Your personal operational picture. Everyone who is not admin or MD lands here instead of Home. |
| **My tasks** | Work assigned to you. |
| **Responsibilities** | Recurring or owned accountabilities (who is on the hook for a theme of work). |
| **Departments** | Org cards, people, and department work. Admin, MD, heads, and managers. Employees do not get this list. |
| **Projects** | Initiatives with teams, milestones, and linked tasks. Always for admin, MD, heads, and managers. For employees, only if they own a project or sit on a project team. |
| **Reports** | Printable / exportable pack. **Company** pack for admin and MD. **Department** pack for heads and managers. Employees do not get Reports. |
| **Activity** | A feed of what changed. You only see events that belong to you or to work you are allowed to see. |
| **Settings** | People and (for admin only) company structure. Admin and MD. Heads invite from **Departments**, not from a full Settings desk. |

Profile menu: alert preferences and **Sign out**.

## 3. Shared rules (every role)

These apply no matter who you are.

**Seeing a task.**  
Admin and MD see all tasks. Heads and managers see tasks in **their department**. Employees see tasks **assigned to them**. If you can see a task, you can **progress** it: status, completion, comments, attachments, deliverables, progress.

**Editing a task** (title, assignee, dates, priority, category, dependencies) is stricter. Admin and MD can edit any task. Heads and managers can edit tasks in their department. An assignee can edit their own task. You cannot edit a colleague’s task in another department unless you are admin or MD.

**Creating work** (new task, project, or responsibility) is for admin, MD, heads, and managers. Employees **request** work instead of creating it.

**Projects.** If you manage the project (admin, MD, project owner, or head/manager of the project’s department), you can change the project, team, milestones, and archive it. If you are only on the team, you work the tasks you can see; you do not reconfigure the project.

**People.** You cannot deactivate your own account. Only admin and MD can activate or deactivate others. Only **admin** can deactivate another admin, and the last admin cannot be removed.

## 4. Workspace Administrator

**Job:** keep the org chart true so everyone else’s desk works. You are not a second MD. You share the company cockpit with the MD, and you alone can change structure.

### You are responsible for

- Departments and teams existing, named correctly, and headed by the right person
- Each person having **one** primary role, a department, a team where needed, and a manager
- Inviting new people at **any** desk, including another administrator or a managing director
- Activating and deactivating accounts when someone joins or leaves
- Not leaving the company with zero administrators

### You can do in WorkHub

- Everything the MD can do on **work**: create tasks, projects, responsibilities; send reminders; resolve leadership requests; turn an employee work-request into a task
- Open **Home** and **Reports** for the **whole company** (export CSV, print)
- Open **Settings** and use **New department**, **New team**, rename department/team, set a department head, **Place a person** (department, team, manager, primary role)
- Invite any role, into any department
- Deactivate people (including another admin, as long as one admin remains)

### You cannot / should not

- Treat “admin” as a shortcut to run a department day to day — that is the head’s job
- Stack yourself as MD as well; one primary role

### How to start the day

Sign in → **Home** (what is overdue or stuck across GCS) → **Settings** when structure is wrong → **Reports** when leadership needs a pack.

## 5. Managing Director

**Job:** run GCS from one cockpit. You see the company. You do not rebuild the org chart; that is the administrator.

### You are responsible for

- Company-wide priorities, stuck work, and overdue work
- Leadership requests from heads (budget, capacity, sign-off)
- Inviting heads, managers, and employees when the function already exists
- Using **Reports** for a company picture you can export or print

### You can do in WorkHub

- **Home**: company completion, overdue, stuck, department scorecard, requests, reminders
- Create and edit work anywhere; manage any project
- Invite **department head**, **manager**, and **employee** (not administrator)
- Activate or deactivate people who are not administrators
- **Reports** at company scope; CSV export and print
- **Settings** for people and roles (no “new department / new team / place a person” forms)

### You cannot

- Create, rename, or re-head departments and teams
- Grant the **administrator** role
- Deactivate an administrator

### How to start the day

Sign in → **Home** → open stuck/overdue items → **Reports** when you need a pack to share → **Departments** when you are following a function.

## 6. Department Head

**Job:** run one function. Your world is **your department** — its people, its work, its report.

### You are responsible for

- Work in your department getting assigned, unblocked, and finished
- Who is on your team (invite **managers** and **employees** into **your department only**)
- Turning employee **work requests** into real tasks when the ask is valid
- Escalating to the MD via a **leadership request** when you need a decision you cannot make
- Your **department report**, not the company totals

### You can do in WorkHub

- Land on **Departments**; open your department card for that department’s tasks
- Create tasks, projects, and responsibilities (new work is tied to **your** department, not another function)
- See and edit tasks in your department; progress any of those tasks
- Manage projects owned by you or belonging to your department
- Invite manager or employee **into your department** (Add person on Departments)
- Submit a leadership request to the MD
- Promote an open work-request into a task
- Open **Reports** for **your department** (export / print that pack)
- See **Activity** for you and for work in your department

### You cannot

- See or edit another department’s full book of work
- Invite into a different department
- Invite an administrator or a managing director
- Deactivate accounts
- See **company** reports or **Home**
- Create departments or teams

### How to start the day

Sign in → **Departments** → your function’s work → **My tasks** for items on you → **Reports** for the weekly pack → **Invite** when someone joins your function.

## 7. Manager

**Job:** assign and move work in the department. You see the same work the head sees. You do **not** hire into the system.

### You are responsible for

- Assigning, retitling, dating, and unblocking tasks in the department
- Creating the tasks and projects the team needs
- The same department report the head can read (you use it; you do not own hiring)

### You can do in WorkHub

- Same visibility as the department head for that department’s tasks and projects
- Create tasks, projects, and responsibilities in your department
- Edit department tasks (not only your own)
- Progress work you can see
- Submit leadership requests
- Promote work-requests into tasks
- **Reports** for the department
- **Activity** scoped like the head’s

### You cannot

- Invite anyone (no Add person)
- Change org structure or deactivate people
- See other departments or company reports
- Open **Settings**

If you need a new person on the team, ask your **department head** (or MD / admin) to invite them.

### How to start the day

Sign in → **Departments** → assign and move work → **My tasks** for what is on you → **Reports** if you are packing the week.

---

## 8. Employee

**Job:** execute assigned work. Ask for more work through the system instead of creating it yourself.

### You are responsible for

- Tasks assigned to you: status, comments, files, deliverables, completion
- Flagging blockers on those tasks
- Using **Request work** when you need something that does not exist yet (it goes to your department head)

### You can do in WorkHub

- **Overview** and **My tasks**
- **Responsibilities** you own (or that you are allowed to take on)
- **Activity** for you and work you can see
- Progress and edit **your** assigned tasks
- **Projects** only if you own a project or are on the project team — then you work linked tasks; you do not reconfigure the project unless you are the owner
- Submit a **work request** (not a free-form new task)

### You cannot

- Create tasks, projects, or responsibilities from scratch
- See Departments, company or department Reports, Home, or Settings
- Invite or deactivate people
- See other people’s tasks unless they are assigned to you
- See Projects until you are on a project

### How to start the day

Sign in → **My tasks** → update status and comments → **Overview** for your picture → **Request work** if you are waiting on work that has not been created.

## 9. Requests: two kinds

| Kind | Who sends it | Who it is for | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Leadership request** | Admin, MD, head, manager | Usually the MD | Decisions, budget, capacity, sign-off. Tracked on Home / request lists. |
| **Work request** | Employee | Department head | “I need this work created.” A head or manager with create-rights can **turn it into a task**. |

Do not use a work request for a company-wide policy decision. Do not use a leadership request to ask for a routine task if you are an employee — use **Request work**.

## 10. Inviting people

| Your desk | Who you may invite | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator | Admin, MD, head, manager, employee | Settings or Departments — any department |
| Managing Director | Head, manager, employee | Settings or Departments |
| Department Head | Manager, employee **in your department only** | Departments (Add person) |
| Manager | Nobody | Ask the head |
| Employee | Nobody | Ask the head |

The invited person receives a workspace account and a **starter password**. They should sign in and keep that password private.

## 12. If you are stuck

| You need… | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| A new department, team, or to move someone between functions | Workspace Administrator |
| A new head, or a company-wide decision | Managing Director |
| A new person on your team | Department Head (or MD / admin) |
| A task that does not exist yet | Employee: **Request work**. Head/manager: create the task. |
| Access you think you should have | Administrator — check role and department placement |

WorkHub will hide screens and refuse actions that are not on your desk. That is intentional. The table in section 11 is the source of truth for what “should” appear.
